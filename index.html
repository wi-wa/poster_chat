About the project

Annulus studies potential LLM consciousness by reducing confounds in training. We train language models from scratch with filtered pretraining data and carefully designed post-training, aiming to reduce the influence of human reports of consciousness and prescribed answers about the models themselves.

How steering works
Each token's J-lens vector, from the selected model's lens, is the direction in a block's output that the lens reads as that token. It is scaled to the strength times the block's typical output norm and added to the outputs of these blocks (block k ends at layer 4k) at every position, while the model reads your messages and writes its reply. Several tokens add up. A typed word is read with a leading space, as inside a sentence; the + beside a J-lens token steers with exactly that token. With the J-lens box open, readouts show the steered model.
